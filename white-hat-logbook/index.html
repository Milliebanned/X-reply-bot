FAQ

Answers

The questions most beginners ask before week 1.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. PortSwigger's lessons and labs are free, Burp Suite Community Edition is free, and so is this logbook. You'd only pay at the very end if you take the BSCP exam ($99), which also needs Burp Suite Professional.

Which browser do I use?

Read lessons in any browser. Chrome is fine. Do labs in Burp's own browser (Proxy → Open browser), because Burp can only see that browser's traffic.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The plan teaches the SQL, Linux and JavaScript you need the week before each topic that uses them. Python comes later, in weeks 15–25.

How much time does it take?

About an hour a day, or 5–9 hours a week. Weeks 15–40 are the heaviest.

What if I fall behind?

The logbook tells you how many weeks behind you are, and weeks 52–65 are spare buffer weeks. Just carry on from your current task.

Is this legal?

Yes. You only attack PortSwigger's practice labs, which exist to be hacked. Never test a real website without the owner's written permission.

Where is my progress saved?

Automatically, every time you tick a step or log time. The badge at the top of the logbook shows where it's saved.

What do I get at the end?

The skills for junior web security jobs and bug bounty programs, and the option to take the Burp Suite Certified Practitioner exam.

Chrome

PortSwigger Academy

The school. Free lessons, plus practice websites called labs that you're allowed to hack. Read lessons in Chrome, or even on your phone.

Burp app

Burp Suite

Your toolkit. An app on your computer that shows every message your browser sends to a website, and lets you change it before it's sent.

Burp's browser

Burp's own browser

Opens from inside Burp: Proxy → Open browser. It's connected to Burp, so Burp can see its traffic. Normal Chrome isn't. Use it for every lab.

The one rule: reading → Chrome. Hacking a lab → Burp's browser, with Burp open.
